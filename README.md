# Training Room


By JinJanMcFlan. A Windows app for Rocket League practice.


**Early test build. Online sign-in is for invited testers.** Some parts are unfinished and marked Coming soon, WIP, Alpha, or Experimental.

## Start here


1. Once a release is available, open **Releases** and look under **Assets**. Get the file named **Training-Room-Setup...exe**. Do not choose Source code.
2. Open your Downloads folder and double-click the setup file. Let installation finish. If Windows shows a warning you do not understand, stop and ask JinJanMcFlan.
3. Open Training Room. Click **Sign in with Discord**. Use your own account, then return to the app. Your account must be on the test invite list.
4. In **Settings → Sound & voice**, turn prompts and cues on, and try **Hear sample**. You can leave the microphone off for your first test.
5. In **Home**, choose the mechanics you want to practice. Open the **Warm-up** card, choose Normal, review the shots, and start.
6. Open Rocket League Free Play. Try the shot shown in Training Room, then use **Hit** or **Miss** in the app.
7. Click **Finish session** in Active View. Open **Progress** to see your results.


## Keys and overlay


The usual first-mechanic keys are **Z = Hit** and **X = Miss**. Hold **Shift** for left or **Alt** for right. When **Typing mode** is on, also hold **Ctrl**. Check the app for your current key choices.


**Ctrl + Shift + Space** shows or hides the overlay. Borderless mode is the best first test. Fullscreen behavior still needs real-game testing.


## Updates and help


Open **Settings → Updates → Check for updates**. Finish your session before restarting to install an update. Update history shows what changed.


If the app says server unreachable, ask whether the test server is running. Reinstalling usually will not fix an offline server.


For help, tell JinJanMcFlan what you clicked and share a picture of the problem. Do not send passwords or sign-in codes.
