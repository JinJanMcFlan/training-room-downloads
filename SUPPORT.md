# Training Room — help and testing

Training Room is an early test app from **JinJanMcFlan**. Support and privacy contact: **jinjanmcflan@gmail.com**.

For a bug report, include your app version, what you were doing, what you expected, what happened, and whether it happens again. A cropped screenshot can help. Remove private messages, account details, access codes and unrelated desktop information first. Never send your Discord password or token. Send sensitive reports by email rather than a public community post.

Features marked **Experimental**, **Alpha** or **Coming soon** are unfinished. A detected goal does not confirm that you performed the selected mechanic correctly. Check your results and keep a copy of important history. You can turn off microphone logging and experimental capture.

Download from the project download link supplied with your invitation. Use the app's Updates page to check your version and available releases. Discord sign-in should open Discord's own authorization page; Training Room should never ask you to type your Discord password into an app form. Only basic account identity is requested for ordinary sign-in; optional integrations ask for extra permissions separately.

Training Room is an independent project, not an official Rocket League or Discord product. Third-party services have their own rules. Free access does not mean the app's source code is open source. Privacy details are available in **Privacy for invited testers**.
